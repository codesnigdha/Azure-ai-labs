# My Learning – Information Extraction

## 🧠 What I Learned

In this lab, I got the opportunity to explore **Azure Content Understanding**
and understand how AI can be used to work with documents and images.

I learned how OCR can extract text from an image, how AI can understand the
layout of a document, and how specific information can be extracted from
receipts.

One thing I understood from this lab is that AI is not just about generating
text. It can also help make sense of unstructured documents and turn the
information into a more organized and usable format.

## 💭 My Experience

I found this lab quite interesting because I was able to work with actual
documents and see how AI processes them.

Before this lab, I mainly thought of document processing as extracting text
from a file. After working with Content Understanding, I understood that AI
can go further by identifying the structure of a document and extracting
specific information from it.

Seeing the analysis results directly in Microsoft Foundry made the concepts
much easier for me to understand.

## 🔍 What I Found Interesting

The **Layout analyzer** was one of the parts I found most interesting. It
showed me that AI can understand how information is arranged in a document,
not just read the text.

I also found **Receipt Analysis** interesting because it demonstrated a
practical use case where information from a real-world receipt can be
identified and presented in a structured way.

## 💻 My Coding Experience

Along with exploring the features in Microsoft Foundry, I also looked at
how these capabilities can be used programmatically with Python.

I worked with examples for:

- OCR / document analysis
- Layout analysis
- Receipt analysis

This gave me a better idea of how the tools I used in the Foundry interface
can also be integrated into applications using the **Azure Content
Understanding Python SDK**.

## 📌 My Key Takeaways

- I learned the basics of Azure Content Understanding.
- I understood how OCR can be used to extract text.
- I learned how AI can understand document layouts.
- I explored how information can be extracted from receipts.
- I understood how unstructured information can be converted into a more
  structured format.
- I got my first hands-on experience with the Azure Content Understanding
  Python SDK.

## 🎯 My Overall Experience

Overall, this lab helped me understand a practical side of AI that I had
not explored much before.

It showed me how AI can reduce the effort involved in processing documents
by automatically reading, understanding, and extracting useful information.

For me, the biggest takeaway was understanding that **AI can do more than
just generate content—it can also understand and organize existing
information.**
