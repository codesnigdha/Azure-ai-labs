# My Learning – Microsoft Foundry

## 🧠 What I Learned

This was my first hands-on lab with **Microsoft Foundry**, and it gave me
a basic understanding of how different Azure AI capabilities can be
explored from one platform.

I learned how to create a Foundry project, explore different models,
deploy a model, and test it using the Playground.

I also got an introduction to **Generative AI, Speech, Computer Vision,
Text Analysis, and Information Extraction**.

## 💭 My Experience

Since this was my first practical experience with Microsoft Foundry, I
found it really helpful to actually explore the platform instead of
only learning about it theoretically.

The **Model Playground** was one of the parts I enjoyed because I could
try different prompts and immediately see how the model responded.

It made the concepts feel much more practical and easier for me to
understand.

## 🔍 What I Found Interesting

What I found most interesting was having so many different AI
capabilities available within the same platform.

I particularly enjoyed exploring **Generative AI and Computer Vision**.
Working with both text and images helped me understand that AI can be
used for very different types of tasks.

## ⚠️ Challenges I Faced

Initially, I found it a little confusing to understand the different
AI services and which one would be suitable for a particular task.

As I explored the different features and worked through the hands-on
activities, I gradually started understanding the purpose of each
service better.

## 📌 My Key Takeaways

- I learned the basics of Microsoft Foundry.
- I learned how to create and work with Foundry projects.
- I explored different AI models and model deployment.
- I practiced interacting with models through prompts.
- I got an introduction to Text Analysis, Speech, and Computer Vision.
- I explored the basics of Information Extraction.
- I gained my first hands-on experience with Azure AI services.

## 🎯 My Overall Experience

Overall, this lab was a good starting point for my **Azure AI learning
journey**.

It helped me become more comfortable with Microsoft Foundry and gave me
an idea of how different AI services can be used for different
purposes.

Most importantly, it helped me move from **just knowing about AI
concepts to actually exploring and working with them**.
