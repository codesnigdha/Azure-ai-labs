# My Learning – Speech in Microsoft Foundry

## 🧠 What I Learned

In this lab, I got to explore how **speech and AI agents can work
together** using Microsoft Foundry.

I learned how an AI agent can listen to spoken questions, understand
the input, and respond using speech. I also got introduced to **Azure
Speech and Voice Live** and how they can be used to make interactions
with AI feel more natural.

## 💭 My Experience

I really enjoyed this lab because, instead of simply typing questions
to an AI, I was able to **actually talk to the AI agent using my
voice**.

It was interesting to see how my speech was converted into something
the AI could understand and how the response was then generated as
spoken audio.

This made me understand the practical difference between a normal
text-based chatbot and a voice-enabled AI experience.

## 🔍 What I Found Interesting

The **Voice mode** was probably the most interesting part of this lab
for me.

Being able to ask a question by speaking and then hear the AI respond
made the interaction feel much more natural and conversational.

I also found the **conversation transcript** useful. After ending the
session, I could see the conversation as text, which helped me
understand how the spoken interaction was captured.

## 💻 What I Explored

Along with using the Voice mode in Microsoft Foundry, I also looked at
the client code used to connect to the AI agent.

This helped me understand that these speech capabilities are not only
available through the Foundry interface but can also be accessed and
integrated into applications using code.

## 📌 My Key Takeaways

- I learned how speech can be integrated with AI agents.
- I explored Azure Speech and Voice Live.
- I experienced speech recognition through hands-on practice.
- I explored how AI responses can be converted into speech.
- I understood how voice input and output can make AI interactions
  more natural.
- I learned how a speech conversation can be captured as a transcript.
- I got an introduction to the client-side code used to access a
  speech-enabled AI agent.

## 🎯 My Overall Experience

Overall, this was a really interesting lab because I got to **experience
AI through voice rather than just text**.

It helped me understand how speech technologies can make AI applications
more interactive and user-friendly. This lab also made me more
interested in exploring how voice-enabled AI agents can be used in
real-world applications.
