# My Learning – Computer Vision

## 🧠 What I Learned

This lab gave me an opportunity to explore **Computer Vision and
Generative AI** using Microsoft Foundry.

I learned how AI can understand images and provide information about
what is present in them. I also explored how AI can generate completely
new images and videos based on simple text prompts.

This helped me understand how AI can work with visual information and
not just text.

## 💭 My Experience

I really enjoyed this lab because it was one of the more visual and
interactive parts of my training.

Until this lab, I had mostly worked with text-based AI features. Here,
I got to upload images, ask questions about them, generate images from
my own prompts, and explore video generation.

Seeing the results directly in Microsoft Foundry made the concepts much
easier for me to understand.

## 🔍 What I Found Interesting

The **image analysis** part was particularly interesting to me. I could
provide an image and use a prompt to ask the model about its contents,
and it was able to understand and describe what was present.

I also enjoyed experimenting with **image generation**. It was
interesting to see how changing the wording of a prompt could change
the generated result.

The **video generation** activity was another new experience for me. It
helped me understand how generative AI is moving beyond text and images
and can also be used to create dynamic visual content.

## 💻 My Coding Experience

Along with exploring these features through the Microsoft Foundry
Playground, I also looked at how they can be used programmatically.

I worked with examples involving:

- Python and the OpenAI SDK
- Image analysis
- Image generation
- REST API for video generation

This helped me understand that the AI capabilities I was using through
the Playground can also be integrated into applications through code.

## 📌 My Key Takeaways

- I learned how AI can analyze and understand images.
- I explored how text prompts can be used for image generation.
- I got an introduction to AI-based video generation.
- I learned how the OpenAI SDK can be used with AI models.

- I understood how computer vision and generative AI can be used
  together in applications.

## 🎯 My Overall Experience

Overall, this was one of the labs I enjoyed the most because I could
actually **see the capabilities of AI in action**.

It helped me realize that AI is not limited to chatbots and text

This lab gave me a better understanding of **Computer Vision and
